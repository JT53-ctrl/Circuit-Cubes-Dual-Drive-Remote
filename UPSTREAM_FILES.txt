ble_lwp.js is functionally the upstream LEGO remote implementation.
ble_nus.js is based on the upstream implementation but has been made
Promise-based and uses a dedicated bleNusBusy flag so Dual Drive commands
are written sequentially and do not conflict with the LEGO remote's
bleBusy flag.
